Random Chore Notification picks a small chore from your own list and nags you to do it at random moments during the day.

• Build your own chore list and set how many chores you get per day
• Choose the active hours and active days
• Full-screen chore prompt with a Start button and a countdown, so "Done" only unlocks once you've really spent the time
• Skip to another chore, or snooze for 10 minutes
• Track chores done today and your daily streak
• Pause for one hour from a launcher shortcut
• Export and import your chore list
• Works offline: no account, no ads, no tracking, no internet permission

Because reminders have to show up on time and open on their own, the app asks for exact alarms, full-screen notifications, display over other apps, and an exemption from battery optimization. You can grant them from the "Finish setup" section.
